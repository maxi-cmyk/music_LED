Each matrix row asks about one frequency

A frequency has both cosine alignment and sine alignment. We need both measurements because the same tone can begin at different phases.

The real part stores the cosine measurement. The imaginary part stores the sine measurement. The sound is still real; is bookkeeping that keeps amplitude and phase together.
